Our story

Born at our kitchen table

Hour Time Games is a family-owned game company. Every game starts as an idea at our own kitchen table, then gets brainstormed, prototyped, and play-tested over laughter-filled family get-togethers.

We believe some of life’s most cherished memories are made playing games together. Game night isn’t just fun and games — it’s a chance to unplug and truly connect with the people who matter most.

Good questions

Frequently asked

I have young kids — do you have any games suitable for a 5 year old? +

YES! Our KEEPER CHAOS game is designed with young children in mind. Its colorful and whimsical animal characters keep even the youngest captivated and excited to play. And we think CLUCK, CLUCK, MOOSE! is perfect for children as young as 4.

I lead a busy life and don’t have the patience for long, difficult games — what about yours? +

We’re a family like yours and understand you want to get right to it. That’s why we designed our games to be quick to learn but to deliver lots of fun memories.

Can I buy your games at the big retailers? +

Yes — our games are available in our own online store, and Cluck, Cluck, Moose! is on Amazon and Walmart too.
